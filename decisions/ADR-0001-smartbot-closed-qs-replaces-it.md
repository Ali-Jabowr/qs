# ADR-0001: SmartBot Industrial is closed; QS replaces it

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-01 · Ali · backfilled 2026-09-21 from CLAUDE.md §7

## Context

SmartBot Industrial — computer vision for PPE and safety compliance — reached no customers, missed
its Gate 1, and had an empty pipeline. QS has one client with a verbal intent to pay.

## Decision

Close SmartBot. Do not revive it and do not cite its gates. Carry over only its lessons, which live
in CLAUDE.md §6: build only on hardware that can be re-ordered (Hikrobot, Intel), never itemise
hardware in a quote, never identify individuals.

## Consequences

- All engineering effort goes to QS.
- The constraints paid for on SmartBot are standing rules here, not suggestions.
